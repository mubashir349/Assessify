Fullscreen is required

The test opens in fullscreen and stays there for the full duration. This is a browser requirement, not optional.

🔒
Exiting fullscreen locks your attempt immediately

Pressing Esc, switching tabs, or minimizing the window freezes your progress and remaining time instantly. Only your test proctor / Institution Admin can unlock it — you cannot resume it yourself.

📶
If you lose connection

Your answered questions are saved. Reconnect and the system restores your attempt automatically.

🏁
Time runs out automatically

If the time limit or window elapses while you're still attempting, your test is submitted automatically using what you've answered so far.
