What Assessify does

Everything a test day needs

AI questions from your PDFs

Upload notes or past papers. The AI drafts MCQ and true/false questions; nothing reaches the bank until you approve it.

Your fields and departments

Set up your own fields, departments and subjects, for Undergraduate, Masters and PhD admissions.

Fixed or flexible timing

Everyone starts together, or candidates start any time in a window. A late starter's timer still ends on time.

A different paper for each candidate

Every candidate gets a random paper from the approved pool. Correct answers never leave the server.

Fullscreen lock and live monitor

Leaving fullscreen locks the test. Admins watch every candidate live and unlock them when needed.

Reports and result emails

Search and filter results, export CSV, email results to candidates and get AI insights on weak topics.
